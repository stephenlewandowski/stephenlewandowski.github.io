Korean Peninsula Aedes suitability.

An audited, reproducible analysis connecting modeled seasonal suitability, ambient population exposure, and source-reviewed South Korean observations—while keeping each evidence class and its interpretation limits distinct.

01 · Project summary

A bounded regional evidence product.

This project examines how modeled environmental suitability for Aedes aegypti and Aedes albopictus changed across North Korea and South Korea from 1975 through 2024. It compares fixed early, middle, and recent decades on the source 0.25° grid and publishes the results as static, inspectable maps and tables.

The analysis adds an audited LandScan ambient-population join and keeps 54 reviewed ROK presence-only records separate from six effort-aware pooled trap-index sites. That separation prevents modeled suitability, population overlap, observed occurrence, and relative trap indices from being presented as interchangeable evidence.

03 · Public products

Results that can be inspected and rebuilt.

  • Results site — responsive maps and summaries for suitability, population exposure, and ROK evidence.
  • Data and products — downloadable SVG and PNG figures, compact CSV and JSON summaries, and a checksummed product manifest.
  • Methods and interpretation — source-grid selection, aggregation, audit controls, evidence semantics, uncertainty, and use limits.
  • Versioned repository — Python analysis, validation, publication builds, audit scripts, metadata, protocols, and derived products.

04 · Practical value

A transparent basis for the next research decision.

The seasonal and population-overlap products can help frame regional surveillance timing, field-validation priorities, and data-quality investments. The public site makes those uses inspectable without implying neighborhood, facility, or individual precision.

The project also makes the remaining evidence gaps explicit: no direct DPRK observations, no reviewed longitudinal event-level Aedes table with non-detections and consistent effort, and no per-cell uncertainty layer in the suitability release. Those gaps must be addressed before formal validation or operational early-warning claims.

Interpretation boundary

This is a regional environmental-suitability and population-exposure analysis. It is not an installation-level product, a disease-risk model, a transmission forecast, or proof of mosquito presence outside the reviewed ROK observations.

Sources and attribution

Built from public, versioned evidence.

Primary inputs include the Climademic Suitability Model dataset, version 2.0 (Siddiqui et al., 2026); the LandScan Mosaic Annual Global Ambient Population Time Series, version 1.0 (Zimmer et al., 2026); Natural Earth country boundaries; and reviewed ROK occurrence and trap-index sources documented in the repository metadata. Full source, licensing, and exclusion records are maintained with the analysis.
