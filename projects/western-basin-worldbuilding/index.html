01 · Overview

Follow the systems that shape a place.

The project begins with real western Lake Erie geography and the connected water, ecology, agriculture, energy, freight, and public infrastructure systems around Toledo and the Maumee watershed. It uses those relationships for scenario development and technology-convergence futures, then translates selected possibilities into illustrated worldbuilding and an evolving Atlas.

02 · Reading boundary

Keep evidence, scenarios, and fiction distinct.

Evidence and model material describe or derive from the real basin. Scenarios explore possible future conditions; fictional canon records deliberate worldbuilding choices. The public Atlas preview labels these categories and includes four visual essays, methods and credits, and a development roadmap.

Current boundary

The Atlas is a public preview, not a completed regional forecast or finished fictional world. The research base and visual language are established; the future-world narrative remains under active development.
