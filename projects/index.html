Featured project

Interactive comparison · Measured air pollution

Hourly PM₂.₅ in 2024

Compare measured hourly PM₂.₅ from 2024 in Seoul, Pyeongtaek, London, Beijing and Los Angeles. Change the averaging period, inspect pollution episodes and see how often concentrations were above a chosen value.

Explore seasonal patterns and concentration distributions, choose among Seoul’s 25 district monitors, and compare one-year statistics with U.S. EPA reference levels. Source notes identify each monitor and show where observations are missing.

Hourly weather · Heat indices

Heat stress in 2024

Compare heat and humidity in the same five cities as the air-quality project.

Explore air temperature, relative humidity, a UTCI radiation scenario and modeled WBGT in Seoul, Pyeongtaek, London, Beijing and Los Angeles. Compare an annual average with daily peaks, sustained hot periods and the number of hours and days above a chosen value.

Monthly and time-of-day patterns show when higher values occurred. The consistent ERA5 source spans all seasons; source notes identify the grid cells and explain the index assumptions.

Featured project

USS Terror (CM-5): Pacific deployment, 1943–1946

My grandfather served aboard USS Terror, but I could not find a campaign map specific to the ship. I reconstructed its deployment in an R/Shiny application with 61 events and 57 route legs. The map distinguishes documented locations from modeled connections and disputed records. The repository includes the spatial-processing code, validation reports, static maps, and QGIS files.

Featured project · audited publication

Korean Peninsula Aedes suitability, 1975–2024.

The analysis compares modeled mosquito seasons across the Korean Peninsula with population data and reviewed observations from South Korea. Each answers a different question about suitability, exposure, or mosquito presence.

The project compares fixed early, middle, and recent decades for Aedes aegypti and Aedes albopictus across 370 source-grid cells. It adds a fully reconciled LandScan population join, 54 reviewed ROK presence-only records, and six separate effort-aware pooled trap-index sites. The publication site provides audited maps, compact data products, transparent methods, and explicit limits on disease-risk or operational interpretation.

Source-linked exhibit · evidence lens

Drinking-water cyber resilience

What do public cyber-incident reports tell us about water-system operations, water safety, and recovery?

The project traces reported intrusions through operational effects and recovery, asking what supports each conclusion about safe water and reliable service. It includes a diagram, a table of supporting sources, and downloadable CSV snapshots. The exhibit separates aggregate reports, the Plymouth municipal case, synthesis, and open questions. A linked teaching brief adds the Minot and Aliquippa cases with their own evidence limits.
