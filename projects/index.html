USS Terror (CM-5): Pacific deployment, 1943–1946

An evidence-aware historical reconstruction combining a 61-event chronology, 57 route legs, an interactive R/Shiny application, reproducible spatial processing, validation reports, static maps, and QGIS exchange files.
