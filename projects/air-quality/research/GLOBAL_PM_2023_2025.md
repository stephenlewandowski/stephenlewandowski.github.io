# London, Los Angeles and Beijing: additional hourly PM₂.₅ records

Prepared on 23 October 2026 (Seoul time) by `acquire_global_multiyear.py`.
All records retain the same monitoring location and instrument stream selected
for 2024. No modeled PM₂.₅, substitute monitor, interpolation or imputation is
introduced. Valid zero readings are retained; negative and non-finite readings
are excluded under the application's existing rule. Every derived JSON contains
the original source URLs, UTC retrieval timestamps and source SHA256 hashes.
Detailed audit files contain output hashes and monthly/seasonal capture.

## LondonAir

Source annual files are `https://londonair.org.uk/r_data/HP1_2023.RData`
and `https://londonair.org.uk/r_data/HP1_2025.RData`. The annual RData format is
the public source used by
[openair::importImperial()](https://github.com/openair-project/openair/blob/main/R/importImperial.R).
R POSIXct instants are formatted in GMT; there is no daylight-saving shift.
The annual object supplies no per-reading ratification or quality flag, and no
ratification claim is made. Concentrations are serialized to three decimal
places, following the 2024 record. The LondonAir API's published reuse terms
identify the Open Government Licence v2; the RData does not embed a separate
license declaration. [LondonAir API](https://www.londonair.org.uk/LondonAir/API/Default.aspx),
[documentation](https://api.erg.ic.ac.uk/AirQuality/Information/Documentation/pdf).

## Los Angeles

The [EPA AirData hourly archives](https://aqs.epa.gov/aqsweb/airdata/download_files.html)
provide `hourly_88101_YEAR.zip`; `annual_conc_by_monitor_YEAR.zip` independently
checks selected sample counts and certification status. Every selected row is
checked for site 06-037-1103, parameter 88101, POC 3, method 170/FEM and local-condition
micrograms per cubic metre. Informational IH/fireworks and IT/US-wildfire flags
are retained, as are IJ/high-wind and J/construction flags in 2025. EPA classifies
all four codes as informational, rather than invalid or request-exclusion flags.
Any unreviewed qualifier or method change stops the acquisition.
[EPA qualifier definitions](https://aqs.epa.gov/aqsweb/documents/codetables/qualifiers.html).
Selected source rows are retained as research CSV.gz extracts, including negative
and flagged observations that permit the transformation to be independently
audited. The source ZIPs remain in the external research cache.

The 2023 annual hourly summary is Certified and reports 8,631 observations.
The app excludes 32 negative values; two IH/fireworks hours are retained.
The 2025 annual hourly summary is also Certified and reports 8,411 observations.
The app excludes 27 negative values; 48 IH/fireworks, 12 IJ/high-winds,
84 IT/US-wildfire and one J/construction hour are retained. Certification does
not override the separate NAAQS comparability qualification below. The genuine
local-year arrays contain 8,599 valid hours in 2023 and 8,384 in 2025.

EPA files are indexed by the *local standard year*. The first eight UTC-year
hours are outside that source file and remain missing; the JSON also preserves
the genuine local-standard year as `localYear`. Supplied UTC timestamps are
checked against every local timestamp plus eight hours. EPA defines these hour
labels as the beginning of sampling. No adjacent-year boundary readings are
invented. See [EPA file formats](https://aqs.epa.gov/aqsweb/airdata/FileFormats.html).

**2025 comparability:** The July 2026 South Coast AQMD network-plan appendix
expressly requests exclusion of Los Angeles Main Street POC 3's 2025 data from
NAAQS comparison after failed bias criteria. The measurements remain useful for
exploratory exposure comparisons but are not an official regulatory assessment.
The site primary monitor is the daily FRM POC 1. The appendix reports a 2023–2025
assessment interval; its text specifically identifies 2025 for the requested
exclusion. No independent EPA approval of that request is claimed.
[South Coast AQMD Appendix C, pages 1–4](https://www.aqmd.gov/docs/default-source/clean-air-plans/air-quality-monitoring-network-plan/aaqmnp-appendix-cA4ED7731453B.pdf).

EPA AQS monitoring data are public domain;
[EPA reuse statement](https://www.epa.gov/outdoor-air-quality-data/do-i-need-request-permission-use-monitoring-data-and-graphics-airdata).

## Beijing

The public [OpenAQ AWS archive](https://docs.openaq.org/aws/year-guide) is listed
without credentials using the prefix
`records/csv.gz/locationid=8833/year=YEAR/`. The script verifies the listing is
not truncated, downloads all daily objects and immediately adjacent boundary-day
objects, and selects only PM₂.₅ sensor 25754. Coordinate and unit consistency are
checked before preparing the unchanged hourly UTC grid. Reported timestamps are
used directly, without rounding. The OpenAQ location page identifies the AirNow
stream as US Public Domain. The CSV archive supplies no ratification or
per-reading quality flags.

The 2023 listing contains 303 daily objects, with a major February–May gap and a
last valid observation on 28 December 07:00 UTC. Coverage is not balanced enough for
a whole-year seasonal comparison, despite 80.1% annual capture. The 2025 listing
contains 63 daily objects and the last valid observation is 4 March 12:00 UTC. No
April–December readings are available from this same monitor. These gaps are
data unavailability, not clean-air periods, and neither annual mean should be
presented as a complete-year city estimate.

## Reproduction and audit

Run `python acquire_global_multiyear.py CITY 2023 2025`, where CITY is `london`,
`los-angeles` or `beijing`. London also requires Rscript. The default raw cache
is `C:/Projects/Public_GitHub/air-quality-comparison/research/extended`.
Source bytes are verified by SHA256, and source ZIPs are checked by their ZIP
CRC integrity checks. Large EPA downloads use verified HTTP byte ranges whose
ordered concatenation reproduces the original source ZIP. Annual outputs have
8,760 slots, UTC midnight 1 January through 23:00 UTC 31 December; no leap-day
assumption is carried over from 2024. All six outputs use schemaVersion 1.
