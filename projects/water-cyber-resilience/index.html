01 · Overview

Separate claims require separate evidence.

The exhibit follows a reported path from cyber access and loss of view or control through operational effects, continuity, water-safety assurance, recovery verification, and justified confidence.

Its central distinction is simple: an intrusion, automation loss, physical consequence, manual continuity, safe-water assurance, recovery, and confidence are not one claim. Each stage has a different evidence requirement.

The evidence lens distinguishes documented aggregate reports, a documented municipal case, bounded synthesis, and an analytical question without treating guidance as incident evidence.

The release is intended for water-sector operators and leaders, public-health and emergency-management professionals, infrastructure-resilience researchers, and advanced students.

04 · Evidence classes

The lens separates records from interpretation.

The consequence path contains different kinds of evidence. The lens makes those distinctions visible rather than turning them into a score or ranking.

  • Documented aggregate: federal incident and advisory records describe reported patterns, not every facility or every technique.
  • Documented case: the Plymouth notice is one municipal continuity and restoration report, not a common forensic dataset.
  • Bounded synthesis: EPA program resources connect assessment, planning, response, and recovery; guidance does not prove implementation.
  • Analytical question: justified confidence remains a question about what evidence operators, regulators, leaders, and the public would need next.

05 · Decision use

What the public record can—and cannot—support.

Supported by the reviewed record

  • Federal and state notices document reported access, configuration changes, operational disruption, and coordinated response activity.
  • A municipal report documents continued manual operation, restored communications, and no reported effect on water levels or water quality at that facility.
  • EPA and NIST resources provide assessment, OT-security, planning, and recovery frames for asking what should be checked next.

Not established by this exhibit

  • Actor attribution, confirmed contamination, or a sector-wide diagnosis.
  • That every reported technique occurred at every water system, or that manual operations are universally safe or sustainable.
  • That restoration alone proves service continuity, water safety, or warranted confidence across systems.

06 · Source boundary

Primary and standards-setting sources.

The exhibit uses eight registered primary or standards-setting sources. Evidence snapshot date: 17 August 2026. Guidance and standards are shown as framework inputs, not incident outcomes.

  • FBI/EPA federal incident notice — aggregate access, reported operational effects, and recovery recommendations.
  • EPA/FBI/CISA/NSA joint advisory — cross-sector OT-disruption categories and resilience recommendations; water-specific scope remains bounded.
  • EPA enforcement alert — domestic risk-and-resilience planning and cybersecurity baseline.
  • Minnesota IT Services response notice — statewide targeting and coordinated response context.
  • City of Plymouth municipal update — one municipal continuity, water-status, restoration, and monitoring report.
  • NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 — OT reliability, safety, topology, threat, and countermeasure frame.
  • EPA cybersecurity assessments — assessment resources and AWIA/SDWA cybersecurity scope.
  • EPA cybersecurity planning — preparation, response, recovery, incident-action, and OT asset-inventory resources.

Interpretation limits

This exhibit does not attribute an actor, report confirmed contamination, diagnose the water sector, evaluate a specific utility's cybersecurity, or claim that manual operations are universally safe or sustainable. Guidance and standards are framework inputs, not proof of implementation or recovery performance. It keeps an aggregated federal pattern separate from one municipal report and treats justified confidence as an evidence question, not a measured result.
