Professional summary

PhD-trained environmental health scientist and university educator with more than 20 years of applied experience in military public health, environmental and occupational health, force health protection, safety, environmental compliance, research, and program leadership. Current work in the Republic of Korea integrates theater-level force health protection with brigade safety and occupational-health oversight.
