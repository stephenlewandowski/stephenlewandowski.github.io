Western Basin Atlas

What might life around Toledo and western Lake Erie feel like in 2075? Explore glass-canopied streets, working farms, industrial yards and a civic Reading Hall through an illustrated regional map and short interactive stories.

Real places and environmental research give this growing world its constraints. Its future buildings and ordinary lives are imagined: a setting for stories, graphic novels and games.

Korean Peninsula Aedes suitability

How has the season suitable for Aedes mosquitoes changed across the Korean Peninsula? Explore 50 years of modeled climate suitability across 370 grid cells, population overlays, and observations from South Korea. Suitability does not establish mosquito presence or disease risk.

About

Science, leadership, and education in applied public health.

My 24-year Army career combined environmental and occupational health, force health protection, water systems, safety, and program leadership. I hold a PhD in Environmental Health Sciences from Columbia, an MS in Environmental Health from Harvard, and a BS in Environmental Science from West Point.

Based in the Republic of Korea, I continue applied research and develop public teaching materials. Earlier full-time teaching appointments include the Uniformed Services University and the United States Military Academy.
