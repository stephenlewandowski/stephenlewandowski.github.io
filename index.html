About

Science, leadership, and education in applied public health.

Stephen A. Lewandowski, PhD, MS, CPH, has more than 20 years of experience across military public health, environmental and occupational health, safety, environmental compliance, program leadership, university teaching, and applied research.

He currently serves as an Environmental Science and Engineering Officer, Force Health Protection Officer, and Brigade Safety Officer with the 65th Medical Brigade. Earlier academic appointments include the Uniformed Services University and the United States Military Academy.

Current project

USS Terror (CM-5): Pacific deployment.

An evidence-aware reconstruction pairs documented chronology with an interactive Shiny application, reproducible spatial analysis, and publication-ready outputs.

R/Shiny · Spatial history · Reproducible research

USS Terror, 1943–1946

The project connects 61 documented deployment events and 57 route legs while keeping source provenance, confidence, date precision, position uncertainty, and the limits of reconstructed routes visible. It also provides mine-warfare evidence review, validation reports, static maps, and QGIS-ready outputs.
