About

Science, leadership, and education in applied public health.

My 24-year Army career combined environmental and occupational health, force health protection, water systems, safety, and program leadership. I hold a PhD in Environmental Health Sciences from Columbia, an MS in Environmental Health from Harvard, and a BS in Environmental Science from West Point.

Based in the Republic of Korea, I continue applied research and teaching as adjunct faculty at the Uniformed Services University and Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University Worldwide. Earlier full-time teaching appointments include the Uniformed Services University and the United States Military Academy.
